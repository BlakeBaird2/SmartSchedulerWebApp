# SmartScheduler Five-Second Test

**Owner:** Grace Farnsworth (First Impressions — entry screens and overarching capability).

Show each participant a SmartScheduler entry screen for five seconds, then ask:

"What does this do?"

**Status:** Two participants recorded so far. Findings are preliminary.

## Participant 1

- **Screen shown:** Entry screen
- **Response:** Understood what SmartScheduler does, but felt unsure where to go next.
- **Did they understand the main purpose?** Yes.
- **Key takeaway:** The main capability was clear, but the next step was not obvious.

## Participant 2

- **Screen shown:** Entry screen
- **Response:** Understood what SmartScheduler does and thought it might take a second to learn, but would become easy to use afterward.
- **Did they understand the main purpose?** Yes.
- **Key takeaway:** The overall purpose was clear, although the interface may require some initial learning.

## Findings and design implications

Both participants understood SmartScheduler's main purpose after viewing the entry screen. However, one was unsure where to go next, and the other anticipated a short learning curve. This suggests that the entry screen communicates the core capability but could make the primary next step more obvious.

**Potential design improvement (not yet implemented from this test):** Make the primary action more prominent and ensure the next step is clear to first-time users.

## Limitations

Only two participants have been recorded so far. These findings are preliminary and may not represent all users.

## Changes made after this test that respond to it

These changes came after the two sessions above and address what they found. They have not been re-tested yet.

- **"Unsure where to go next."** Every entry screen now has exactly one blue main button ("Start planning" on the home page, "Add calendar" in setup, "Done" on Plan). First-time visitors also get a welcome pop-up that gives them a concrete goal and a **Start** button.
- **"Might take a second to learn."** The welcome pop-up offers an optional four-step walkthrough ("Show me around") with a small drawing of each part of the Plan screen. It can be replayed from Settings.
